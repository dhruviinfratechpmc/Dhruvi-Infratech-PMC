Who We Are

Your Dedicated Execution Partner

Dhruvi Infratech PMC is a client-side project management consultancy based in Jaipur, Rajasthan. We act as the owner's dedicated execution partner — translating architectural vision into built reality through disciplined planning, rigorous coordination, and uncompromising quality control.

We do not represent contractors or suppliers. Our loyalty is to the client and the project — protecting your interests at every decision, from concept design through to final handover.

Working on behalf of the client, we bring together architects, structural, MEP, interior, ELV, landscape, and specialist consultants under a single, accountable point of control.

What We Do

End-to-End Project Management

Our scope can be engaged in full or tailored to the stage at which you bring us on board — from first concept sketch to keys handed over.

01
Pre-Construction & Planning
Feasibility review, project brief development, master programme preparation, budget estimation, value engineering, cash-flow planning, and consultant appointment coordination.
02
Design Management & Coordination
Review and coordination of Architectural, Structural, MEP, Interior, ELV, and Landscape drawings. Clash detection, RFI management, drawing register control, and revision tracking.
03
Tendering & Procurement
Tender documentation, contractor and vendor recommendation, contract structuring and negotiation support to protect the client commercially.
04
Construction Supervision & QA/QC
Site supervision, progress monitoring, method and material approvals, quality assurance and quality control aligned to specifications, codes, and brand standards.
05
Cost, Schedule & Risk Management
Schedule management, milestone tracking, delay-risk mitigation, bill verification, variation control, budget reconciliation, and proactive contingency planning.
06
Compliance & Handover
Snagging, de-snagging, commissioning oversight, O&M manual compilation, statutory approvals, and final project closeout including defect liability management.
